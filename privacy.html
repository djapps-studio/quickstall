QUICKSTALL PRIVACY POLICY

1. Overview

QuickStall is designed to keep as much application and business information on your device as reasonably possible. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information Stored on the Device

QuickStall may store configured products, prices and discounts, inventory, orders and order history (including saved item and order discounts), payment and delivery status, optional order Custom IDs, business and regional settings, UPI or payment configuration, imported merchant QR images, device-pairing and synchronization state, backup settings, and a locally protected paid-access tier. Paid-access records may include separately verified legacy lifetime and Pro-subscription product identifiers, non-reversible purchase-token hashes, verification and clock timing used to limit temporary offline access, and a protected subscription base-plan hint and automatic-renewal state when available. A separate authenticated record may store only that this installation previously held verified paid access or a verified demo so saved Order History can remain visible after a downgrade. That record grants visibility only; neither it nor a base-plan hint grants a paid tier or paid processing. An optional Custom ID is generic text chosen by the user, stored with the order, and may contain a name, phone number, token number, table number or another reference. Purchase tokens, the protected paid-access cache and the stored-data-visibility record are not included in QuickStall backups. Legal acceptance is stored locally in application preferences.

Appearance choices, tutorial progress and whether the optional Pro introduction has been dismissed stay on this phone. They are not sent to paired devices or included in QuickStall backups, Android cloud backup or device transfer. The introduction does not record trial eligibility or start a subscription. Tutorial illustrations use demonstration data and do not create orders, payments or purchases.

The last in-app update reminder time stays on this phone and is excluded from business synchronization, QuickStall backups, Android cloud backup and device transfer.

3. Data Sent Off the Device

In demo-enabled builds, ordinary launches, foreground changes and premium actions check locally held signed certificates without contacting the licensing backend, starting Google sign-in or requesting Play Integrity. Protected clock checkpoints stay on this installation and are excluded from backups and synchronization. Verification failures may record only fixed technical rejection categories and verdict labels or flags, without tokens, account identifiers or business data. Viewing Pro can query Google Play plan and ownership metadata. Explicit activation, account verification, purchase or restore can contact Google sign-in, Play Integrity, Google Play and the dedicated QuickStall licensing service hosted on Google Cloud/Firebase in Mumbai where supported. Google identity tokens are verified for signature, audience, issuer, expiry and request nonce. Google identity tokens can contain an email address, name and profile claims; raw tokens and unused claims are processed transiently for verification. When support-directory collection is enabled, updated clients disclose and sign the directory version during an explicit verification. The service then retains the verified sign-in email, a Support ID derived from the existing keyed account identifier, directory creation and verification times, original demo dates, and a limited snapshot of verified purchase product, plan, state, paid-through date, renewal setting, test-payment flag and Play order reference when available. It does not store a display name or raw Google subject. Older clients do not opt into this directory. Only authorized administrators can search the directory through a separate read-only console; the sign-in email may differ from the Google Play purchasing account. A Support ID is a support reference, not proof of ownership or an access credential. These identifying directory records and purchase snapshots are kept for account support until verified deletion; a later explicit account verification can create a directory record again. The service uses the Google account identifier and verified-email claim, installation public key and signed proof, Integrity token, product identifiers and purchase tokens when restoring/verifying purchases. The keyed identifier, original demo dates and purchase-ownership hashes remain the licensing authority; the identifying support directory is separate and contains no raw identity or purchase tokens. Network infrastructure processes IP addresses and technical request metadata; the application hashes IPs only in bounded in-memory rate limits. Business records, Custom IDs, inventory, payment configuration and backups are not sent to this service. Identity, Integrity and purchase tokens are processed transiently and excluded from application logs. Signed certificates, installation private keys, protected clock anchors and retry state remain device-local and are excluded from backups and device transfer. The installation private key stays in Android Keystore. QuickStall queries the Pro subscription and legacy lifetime products separately during explicit restore. Existing builds without hybrid licensing retain their original Google Play refresh behavior. Optional Google Drive backup uses Google authorization and sends a QuickStall backup, which may include orders and Custom IDs, to the private application-data area of the Google account you select. Password-protected cloud backups are encrypted by QuickStall before upload. Google-account-only backups do not add user-held QuickStall encryption and rely primarily on Google account authorization, Drive protections, the private application-data area and QuickStall integrity checks. Nearby Connections uses Google Play services and local wireless technologies to discover, authenticate and synchronize paired QuickStall devices, including order Custom IDs when device sync is enabled. Android and Google services may process technical information as needed to provide those services.

Google Play in-app updates check application update availability and installation status when Home opens or you choose Check for updates in Settings. Google Play handles the update download and installation after your consent and processes device metadata, application version and the list of installed modules and asset packs to determine update availability and size. QuickStall does not send orders, Custom IDs, payment configuration or backups through the update API.

QuickStall does not request phone, contacts or SMS permissions and does not automatically read or collect phone numbers. A user may choose to type a phone number or other identifying information into an order's generic Custom ID field. QuickStall does not include an application-operated analytics, advertising or tracking service.

Receipt preview is generated locally from the selected saved order. Choosing Share PDF sends a PDF of the preview to the application you select; Share text sends its text. Delivery order details, including completed deliveries, can also be previewed, shared or printed with their displayed items, total, merchant-recorded payment status and delivery state. Custom ID is excluded unless selected. Choosing Print / PDF passes a generated receipt document to Android's print system and your selected print service or PDF destination. It includes order items, prices, discounts, currency, time, order reference, origin label and recorded payment balance. Custom ID is excluded by default and included only when selected in the preview. Those destinations may retain or transmit the receipt under their own policies. QuickStall does not automatically upload receipts or add a customer database.

Sales summaries are calculated locally from saved non-imported orders. Choosing Save CSV or Save PDF writes item names, quantities, gross amounts, item and allocated order discounts, total discounts, sale amounts, currencies, selected dates, time zone and report notes to the destination selected through Android's document picker. Choosing Share PDF sends the complete report to the application you select. Choosing Print passes the same report to Android's print system and the selected print service. Reports exclude order IDs, Custom IDs, device identifiers and payment configuration; no tax amount is inferred. Those selected apps, document providers and print services may retain or transmit the report under their own policies. QuickStall does not automatically upload summaries, retain a separate report history or request broad storage access.

Shared PDFs are generated in private temporary cache, outside business backups. Only the selected file receives temporary read access through Android. Files are kept for delayed reads by receiving apps; copies older than 24 hours are removed on a later share, and Android may clear cache sooner. Receiving apps and print services may retain or transmit their own copies under their policies. Sharing does not change saved orders, payment or delivery state, or inventory.

Optional product barcodes, categories, favorites and low-stock thresholds are stored with the catalog and included in business backups and authorized Billing Device synchronization. A keyboard-style scanner supplies text input locally; QuickStall does not use the camera or an external barcode lookup service. The selected interface language is device-local and is not included in business synchronization or portable backups. No loyalty profiles or customer contact database are maintained.

4. Payments

UPI or payment-link transactions are completed using your selected payment or banking application or provider. QuickStall does not operate a banking or payment-processing service. QuickStall Pro subscriptions and preserved legacy purchases use Google Play Billing.

The optional 30-day demo has server-held original dates and device-bound signed access; it does not collect a payment method or create a subscription. Existing Google Play subscription trials remain managed by Google Play. Verified demo history can preserve visibility and recovery export after expiry without granting paid processing.

Licensing-data deletion is available through About & Legal and the public web page, including without an active entitlement or installed app. Google sign-in on that page is loaded only after your explicit action and generates a signed 24-hour ownership receipt for you to include in a support request. No email is sent automatically. The receipt contains the keyed account identifier, not your email or raw Google token. Support verifies it before fulfilling deletion. The service retains a keyed account identifier and original demo dates for its lifetime, plus purchase-ownership hashes to prevent reassignment. The verified deletion process removes the identifying licensing directory, its purchase snapshot and account admission records; original eligibility and purchase-ownership hashes remain retained. Admission counters become eligible for deletion after 48 hours; platform cleanup may take longer. Token-free application logs have 14-day retention; required Google Cloud audit logs follow Google's separate retention rules. Deletion does not cancel subscriptions or delete local business data or backups. Contact support.djapps@gmail.com for assistance or an equivalent verified ownership process.

5. Backups

Local backup files and order-history CSV exports you create may contain QuickStall business information, including Custom IDs, saved discounts, order-origin device identifiers and labels, and payment and delivery state. Store and share them carefully. QuickStall records CSV-imported orders as history-only and preserves that designation in later backups and synchronized Billing history. Google Drive backup is optional and kept in the application's private Google Drive data area. Password-protected cloud backups are encrypted by QuickStall before upload; Google-account-only backups are not described as end-to-end encrypted. Google account authorization is handled by Google services.

6. Local Connectivity

When you enable device synchronization, paired QuickStall devices may exchange device identity, products, inventory, orders (including Custom IDs, saved discounts and payment/delivery state), payment configuration and synchronization metadata over encrypted local connections. Nearby permissions are requested only when required for discovery or connection. Removing a paired device revokes its saved relationship but does not replace your responsibility to secure the devices you use.

Pending device removal retains a local pairing credential only for removal-control and connection-liveness messages, not business synchronization. After a five-minute grace period, the next active removal cleanup discards that local credential even if the other device has not acknowledged removal.

Cached delivery orders remain after unpairing until you choose to clear them. Clear orders from unpaired devices deletes the confirmed order copies, their line items and unsent accept or delivery updates from that Delivery Device only. Billing records, other phones, backups and removal-security records are not deleted by this action. Devices that are merely offline or disconnected remain protected; pairing and syncing again may restore local copies.

7. Third-Party Services

QuickStall currently integrates Google Play Billing, Google Play in-app updates, Google Play services Nearby Connections, Google sign-in/Credential Manager, Play Integrity, the dedicated QuickStall Firebase/Google Cloud licensing service, and Google authorization and Drive services for optional cloud backup. The separate operator console uses Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication with Google sign-in for administrators; customer licensing verification does not create Firebase Authentication accounts. Their handling of information is governed by Google's applicable policies and your device or account settings.

8. Security

QuickStall uses reasonable safeguards, including Android application storage, protected local synchronization and encrypted cloud-backup content. No application, wireless link, account or device-storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as QuickStall, its supported features or applicable requirements change. A material legal update may be presented for acknowledgement again.

For support or privacy questions, use the QuickStall developer contact shown in the application's Google Play listing.
